Nullarbor — Mechanical Summary
Planet

## Flavour:

Nullarbor is a desert planet known for tarry deposits and rich in uranium.
It has a native population of uranium-enriched creatures called hunters.
Water needs to be condensed out of the atmosphere where the air is cleanest.
Pollution will drive back the hunters but make condensers stop functioning.

Resources

- Ferrous  patches: drilled, common
- Cupric  patches: drilled, common
- Common rock patches: drilled, abundant
- Uranium rock patches: drilled, rare
- Surface rocks: hand-mined, yield stone (early game)
- Weathered nodules: hand-mined, yield starter iron/copper ore + solid fuel (early game)
- Sand is not mineable
- Uranium removed from Nauvis is NOT done; Nauvis uranium remains vanilla

Retort (primary processing building)

Burner-fueled; fuel is building consumption, not a recipe ingredient
Crafting speed scales with fuel quality
Recipes (the "de-X-ifier"):

Iron-tar → iron ore + solid fuel + crude oil
Copper-tar → copper ore + solid fuel + crude oil
Common rock → U-238 + stone
Uranium rock → U-235 + stone


Tar recipes are net fuel-positive; rock recipes produce no fuel
Unlocked early (first/second research)

Burner Assembler (production building)

2×2 footprint
Burner-fueled; no module slots
Crafting speed scales with fuel quality (~1× coal to ~8× nuclear)
+50% base productivity on "energetic chemistry" recipe family (solid fuels, rocket fuel, premium fuel, explosives, grenades)
Can craft general assembler recipes at base productivity (no bonus)
Refuses recipes outside its allowed set
Designed for dense clustering with shared fuel belts
Uses steel furnace as a crafting ingredient
Single fuel slot, small buffer (requires continuous fuel supply)

Distributor (burner inserter variant)

1×2 footprint; must be placed on a belt (integrates a belt-through segment)
2 drop tiles per long side (4 total), each 1 tile away
Feeds up to 4 adjacent buildings per swing (one drop per building)
Burner-fueled (own fuel slot)
Fuel priority: own fuel slot > fuel for adjacent assemblers > ingredient for adjacent assemblers
Uses vanilla filter-inserter logic for item selection
Speed scales with fuel quality

Gantry (logistics building)

6×4 footprint
Straddles rails; auto-transfers full inventory to/from wagon underneath
Belt I/O on long sides (load/unload)
Fuel slot on short side only; fuel via inserter or bots (bots impractical at scale)
Toggle: Load wagon / Unload wagon / Off (affects wagon subsystem only)
Belt I/O and wagon I/O operate independently, both filling/emptying internal inventory
Internal inventory ~80–120 slots (buffer, not storage)
Transfer speed scales with fuel quality (coal ≈ vanilla stack-inserter parity; nuclear ≈ 4–5× faster)
No fuel consumption when idle
Functions as belt buffer/balancer when no train present (emergent, not optimized)
One wagon cannot be under multiple gantries; alignment tolerant but not exact
Footprint forces fuel-access gaps every 2 wagons → encourages locomotive spacers, short trains (1-1, 1-2), or interleaved consists

Trains

Heavy Freight Locomotive: larger fuel slot, higher top speed, slower acceleration, better fuel efficiency
Combat wagons: mobile turrets, uranium-tipped ammunition
Armored locomotives: depleted uranium (U-238) plating
Locomotive fuel quality affects speed (vanilla mechanic, more impactful at scale)

Pollution (inverted mechanic)

Generated by burner buildings, trains, and dedicated flare stacks
Suppresses hunter initiation within polluted zones
Disables/degrades condensers above a pollution threshold (linear scaling down)
Required to make outposts and rail corridors safe
Dissipates faster than Nauvis (requires continuous generation)

Hunters (enemies)

Territorial; inhabit pristine desert; do not initiate combat in polluted zones
Once engaged, pursue targets into pollution and attack any nearby targets until none remain, then retreat
Do NOT attack rails, power lines, or belts
DO attack player, vehicles/trains, and other buildings
Species/roles:

Rail-hunters: target moving trains
Pack hunters: swarm static infrastructure
Exploding hunters: slow, AOE detonation on death (uranium bioaccumulation), no friendly fire to other hunters, small uranium drop if killed at range without triggering explosion


Density higher in uranium-rich regions
Aggression scales with cumulative industrialization

Water

Atmospheric condensers only (must be in low-pollution areas)
Off-planet water import possible but expensive (rocket throughput)
Creates forced combat engagement (condensers in hunter territory)

Petrochemicals

Crude oil from retort tar recipes → vanilla refinery → light/heavy/petroleum
All downstream petrochemistry uses vanilla chem plant recipes
No new fluid types introduced

Uranium / Nuclear

U-238 from common rock; U-235 from uranium rock (geographically separate)
Direct isotope output (no uranium ore intermediate on Nullarbor)
Centrifuges optional on Nullarbor (Kovarex/reprocessing available but not required)
U-235 uses: nuclear fuel cells, premium fuel ingredient
U-238 uses: armor, ammunition, combat tech (bulk byproduct sink)

Premium Fuel

Top-tier solid fuel; made in burner assembler from refined products + U-235
Uses: max-tier fuel for trains/gantries/burner buildings, exportable to all planets
Also serves as the planet's science pack (consumed by labs)
Creates research-vs-operations resource tension

Science

Premium fuel doubles as the science pack
Labs consume premium fuel for Nullarbor research

Tech Progression

Starter recipe (bootstrap in existing buildings)
Retort + Condenser
Burner Assembler + Distributor
Gantry + Heavy Freight Locomotive
Centrifuge (optional uranium enrichment)
Premium fuel + science pack (capstone)

Outpost Economics

Tar outposts: net fuel producers, self-sufficient
Rock outposts: net fuel consumers, require fuel import
Mixed outposts (tar + rock co-located): economically efficient
Rail network carries fuel inward to rock outposts, materials outward to hubs
Outposts require local pollution generation for defense (burner buildings/flare stacks)

Export Role

Premium fuel (universal fuel upgrade + science)
Uranium isotopes / nuclear materials
Petrochemical products (abundant crude byproduct)
Pre-Aquilo nuclear infrastructure enabler
